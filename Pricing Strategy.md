# Pricing Strategy: Showing Prices vs. CTA-Only Approach

## Executive Summary

**For your AWS & Kubernetes consulting services, showing prices is the RIGHT strategy.**

Why? Your services are:

- ✅ Fixed-scope, productized offerings (not custom consulting)
- ✅ Clear deliverables with defined timelines
- ✅ Target audience is cost-conscious founders/CTOs
- ✅ Prices are reasonable and defensible ($3K-$9.5K for sprints)
- ✅ You want to pre-qualify leads and reduce tire-kickers

**Recommendation:** Keep prices visible with strategic enhancements (detailed below).

---

## The Two Approaches: Detailed Comparison

### Approach A: Show Prices (Current Strategy)

#### Advantages ✅

1. **Pre-Qualification**

   - Filters out prospects who can't afford services
   - Saves time on discovery calls with non-buyers
   - Attracts serious leads who've already budget-approved

2. **Builds Trust**

   - Transparency signals confidence
   - No "bait and switch" concerns
   - Modern buyers expect pricing visibility
   - Reduces perceived risk

3. **Shorter Sales Cycle**

   - Eliminates "what does this cost?" objection
   - Prospects arrive pre-sold on value
   - Discovery call focuses on fit, not price negotiation

4. **Competitive Positioning**

   - Most consultants hide prices (you stand out)
   - Shows you're productized (not hourly billing)
   - Demonstrates you know your value

5. **SEO & Content Benefits**

   - Ranks for "EKS migration cost", "AWS security audit price"
   - Shareable pricing becomes content itself
   - Helps with comparison shopping

6. **Psychological Benefits**
   - Removes mystery/anxiety for buyers
   - Shows respect for their time
   - Positions as professional product, not "depends" consulting

#### Disadvantages ❌

1. **Price Anchoring**

   - Some prospects might be scared off prematurely
   - Can't adjust pricing based on scope complexity
   - Limits negotiation flexibility

2. **Competitive Intelligence**

   - Competitors can see your pricing
   - Might undercut you (race to bottom risk)

3. **Perceived Commoditization**

   - Fixed prices can seem "off the shelf"
   - May reduce perceived customization

4. **Revenue Optimization**
   - Can't price based on client's budget/urgency
   - Might leave money on table for enterprise clients

---

### Approach B: "Contact for Pricing" / CTA-Only

#### Advantages ✅

1. **Pricing Flexibility**

   - Can adjust based on client size, urgency, scope
   - Opportunity to price based on value, not cost
   - Upsell during conversation

2. **Lead Capture**

   - Everyone who's serious must contact you
   - Build email list of interested prospects
   - Opportunity to nurture leads over time

3. **Qualification Conversation**

   - Learn about prospect before quoting
   - Can position against competitors
   - Build relationship before talking money

4. **Premium Positioning**
   - "If you have to ask, you can't afford it" vibe
   - Suggests custom, high-touch service
   - Appeals to enterprise buyers

#### Disadvantages ❌

1. **Friction & Drop-off**

   - 70-80% of interested visitors won't reach out
   - Creates unnecessary barrier to sale
   - Frustrates buyers who want quick answers

2. **Time Waste**

   - Many calls with unqualified leads
   - Budget surprises after investment in discovery
   - Leads ghost after hearing price

3. **Trust Issues**

   - Modern buyers expect transparency
   - Feels like "used car salesman" tactic
   - Creates suspicion about value

4. **Competitive Disadvantage**

   - Competitors with clear pricing win faster
   - Loses comparison shoppers
   - Perceived as old-school consulting

5. **SEO Limitations**
   - Can't rank for price-related searches
   - Less shareable/viral potential
   - Misses "price aware" searches

## Strategic Recommendations for Your Services

### Keep Prices Visible BUT Enhance the Presentation

#### 1. Add "Starting at" Language for Flexibility

**Current:** "Investment: $3,500"

**Enhanced:**

```
Investment: Starting at $3,500
(Scope and timeline customized during discovery call)
```

**Benefits:**

- Maintains transparency
- Allows pricing flexibility
- Sets expectation that actual price may vary
- Opens door for upsells

#### 2. Add Price Justification Elements

For each service, add:

**ROI Calculator:**

```
Cloud FinOps Sprint - $3,500
Typical client saves: $5,000-$15,000/month
Break-even: 1 month
First-year ROI: 20-60x investment
```

**Cost Comparison:**

```
EKS Golden Path Setup - Starting at $8,000
vs. Bad hire: $150,000 + 6 months
vs. Fragile DIY setup: Weekly incidents, opportunity cost
vs. Big consulting firm: $50,000-$100,000
```

**Value Breakdown:**

```
Security Hardening Sprint - $4,500

Includes:
✓ IAM audit & redesign ($1,500 value)
✓ Network hardening ($1,200 value)
✓ SSM implementation ($800 value)
✓ Documentation & runbooks ($1,000 value)
✓ Total deliverable value: $4,500+
```

#### 3. Create Pricing Tiers (Good, Better, Best)

Transform each service into 3 tiers:

**Example: Cloud FinOps Sprint**

**Tier 1: Quick Audit** - $2,500

- AWS cost analysis only
- High-level recommendations
- 2-day turnaround
- DIY implementation

**Tier 2: Standard Sprint** - $3,500 (MOST POPULAR)

- Full cost audit
- EKS rightsizing
- Detailed action plan
- Implementation support

**Tier 3: Full Implementation** - $5,500

- Everything in Standard
- Hands-on implementation
- Automation scripts
- 30-day monitoring

**Benefits:**

- Creates price anchoring (middle tier looks reasonable)
- Allows self-selection
- Natural upsell path
- Shows range without hiding base price

#### 4. Add "Custom Enterprise" Option

At the bottom of pricing section:

```
Need something different?

For teams with unique requirements, compliance needs,
or multi-phase transformations, we offer custom engagements.

→ Schedule a custom scoping call
```

**Benefits:**

- Captures enterprise leads
- Maintains flexibility for large deals
- Doesn't muddy core pricing

#### 5. Show Pricing Early, But Add Friction for Details

**Above the fold (Hero section):**

```
Services starting at $2,500
Fixed-scope engagements, not hourly billing
View detailed pricing ↓
```

**On pricing section:**

- Show base prices for all services
- Add "View detailed breakdown" expand/collapse
- Detailed scope only visible on click

**Benefits:**

- Immediate price awareness
- Doesn't overwhelm visitors
- Engages curious prospects

## Handling Common Pricing Objections

### "Your prices seem high"

**Response Framework:**

```
I appreciate you being direct about budget. Let me show you
the ROI calculation:

For the Cloud FinOps Sprint ($3,500):
- Typical client AWS bill: $15,000/month
- Average reduction: 25% = $3,750/month savings
- You break even in < 1 month
- First year savings: $45,000

So you're not spending $3,500—you're investing $3,500
to save $45,000. That's a 13x return.

Would it help to see a case study from a similar company?
```

### "Why not hire someone full-time?"

**Response Framework:**

```
Great question. Let's compare:

Full-time DevOps engineer:
- Salary: $120K-$150K/year
- Hiring time: 3-6 months
- Ramp-up time: 3 months
- Total cost year 1: $150K+
- Risk: Wrong hire, cultural fit issues

EKS Golden Path Setup ($8,000):
- Started next week
- Done in 2 weeks
- Senior expertise immediately
- No ongoing commitment
- You own everything

For most companies at your stage, the sprint gets you
6-12 months ahead, then you can hire when you're ready.

Does that timeline work better for you?
```

### "Can you discount for multiple services?"

**Response Framework:**

```
Absolutely! I offer bundle pricing when it makes sense:

2 services: 10% discount
3+ services: 15% discount

For example:
- FinOps Sprint: $3,500
- Security Hardening: $4,500
- Observability: $3,000
- Total: $11,000
- Bundle price (15% off): $9,350
- You save: $1,650

Plus, doing them together actually delivers better results
because the systems integrate.

Which combination makes sense for your priorities?
```
